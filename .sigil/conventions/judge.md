# Judge rules: decision quality

Apply these to each named group in `decisions`, in addition to the usual review.
A finding must quote the authored text it is about.

- The `Decision:` states a chosen course, not a wish, a question, or a
  restatement of the goal.
- The `Scope:` names the boundary and the exclusions that matter, so a reader
  can tell what the decision does not govern.
- Each `Discarded alternatives:` entry says why the option was rejected, not
  only that it was.
- `Revisit when:` names a condition someone could observe, not "if needed".
- The `Trade-offs:` name a cost the decision actually accepts.
- The decision may name the course a Constraint binds, but gives the reason for
  it rather than only repeating the constraint's wording, and adds no binding
  commitment that Constraints lack.
- An optional label is present only when it carries content; flag a label that
  restates another label's paragraph.
